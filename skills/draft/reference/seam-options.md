# Choosing between viable seam sets

Read this at step 5a when two or more seam sets are viable, before asking for approval.

When two or more seam sets are viable, first follow the delivery conductor's
**Human decision brief**. Explain what each option catches and misses,
relevant complexity or concurrency consequences, and boundary, coupling,
reliability, and operability effects. Do not invent differences when the seams
are materially equivalent.
